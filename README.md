# DBZ: Stories Untold

A side-scrolling Dragon Ball Z action audiogame, fully playable by sound, for blind and sighted players. Unofficial fan game. Formerly called Saiyan Mercenary.

## The setting

The game is set in an alternate universe. It looks a lot like the timeline you know: Frieza's empire rules the stars, Saiyans are hired out as planet-clearing muscle, and the Frieza Force shows up now and then. But a lot of things play out differently, and many of the places, races and people are new.

In this universe, one more Saiyan survived. His name is Rutaba. **Paid in Blood** is his story.

## What it is

- Fights on a line at three heights (ground, mid-air, high), with a distinct wind-up sound for every attack: block, parry, dodge and counter by ear
- Ki blasts, charged techniques with spoken chants, beam struggles, air dashes, Super Saiyan and the Great Ape
- Two paths, Primal and Divine, shaped by your choices and your fighting style
- A world that remembers what you did
- A space pod between chapters with a training simulator, minigames, space missions, uncharted worlds, Saibaman seeds and secrets
- Speech through your screen reader (NVDA, JAWS and others) or the Windows voice; keyboard or Xbox controller

## What's playable

The prologue, a basics tutorial, and chapters one to three: Thessk, Hoarfell and the Vask Open.

## Planned

Chapters four to seven finishing Rutaba's story, then side stories for other characters in this universe.

## Download

Get the zip from [Releases](../../releases), unzip it anywhere, and run `DBZStoriesUntold.exe`. Headphones are strongly recommended. The full manual is `readme.txt` inside the zip.
